Lab 5 • Non-Preemptive

First-Come, First-Served

Processes are executed in exact order of arrival. Simple FIFO queue, zero overhead, but can cause convoy effect.

Complexity: O(N) Preemptive: No
Lab 5 • Non-Preemptive
⚡

Shortest Job First (SJF)

Executes the available process with the smallest CPU burst time. Mathematically proves minimum average waiting time for batch jobs.

Criterion: Min Burst Time Preemptive: No
Lab 6 • Preemptive SJF
🔄

Shortest Remaining Time Next

Preemptive version of SJF. Preempts the running process whenever a new job arrives with a shorter remaining burst time.

Criterion: Min Rem Time Preemptive: Yes ⚡
Lab 6 • Preemptive
🔁

Round Robin (RR)

Each process is assigned a cyclic CPU time quantum. Excellent responsiveness for time-sharing systems with zero starvation.

Time Slice (Q): Configurable Preemptive: Yes ⚡

Priority Scheduling (NP)

CPU is allocated to the highest priority process. Runs until completion without interruption from arriving processes.

Criterion: Priority Level Preemptive: No
